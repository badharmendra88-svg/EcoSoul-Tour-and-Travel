# Website photo sources

Photos set on 23 Oct 2026 for the homepage hero, destinations and packages. Every one except Mukteshwar (see "Credited photos" below) is **public domain or CC0**, so it can be used commercially without a credit line. This file records where each came from, in case anyone asks.

Each licence was checked directly on its Wikimedia Commons file page on 23 Oct 2026: licence CC0 / public domain, "attribution required: no", no restrictions, and no deletion, copyright or personality-rights warnings. Two photos (Jim Corbett, Kumaon Scenic Circuit) are Unsplash imports whose Commons licence review is still pending; the Unsplash licence also allows free commercial use without credit, so they are fine either way.

Change or remove any of them in **Admin → Photos** (Super Admin only). The app converts uploads to WebP (max 2000 px). Ranikhet was cropped to remove a camera date stamp, and Rishikesh was cropped to a wide frame around the temple and river.

## Credited photos

**Mukteshwar** (added 24 Oct 2026) is the one exception to "no credit needed". The only modern CC0 photo of Mukteshwar is a branded hotel, so a CC BY 2.0 photo is used instead. Its credit line ("Photo: Sanjoyg, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons") is stored in the photo's credit field and shown on the destination page. Keep that credit if the photo stays, or replace the photo with your own.
